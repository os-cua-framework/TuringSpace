## Photo packs

- `assets/openimages_spatial/` — Open Images V7 validation photographs with their bounding-box and visual-relationship annotations. Images are listed as **CC BY 2.0**; annotations are **CC BY 4.0**. Per-image author, original landing page and license are recorded in [`openimages_spatial/README.md`](openimages_spatial/README.md).
- `assets/coco_grid/` — COCO val2017 photographs (**CC BY 2.0** or **CC BY-SA 2.0**, per image) and their instance polygons (**CC BY 4.0**); see [`coco_grid/README.md`](coco_grid/README.md).
